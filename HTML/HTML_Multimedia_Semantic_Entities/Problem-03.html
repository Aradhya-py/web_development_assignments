Travel Tips

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Visit the Taj Mahal in the morning.
  2. Explore Agra Fort.
  3. Have lunch at a local restaurant.
  4. Visit Itmad-ud-Daulah.
  5. Watch the sunset near Mehtab Bagh.

Travel Guide

Plan your visits according to opening hours and weather conditions. Carry water and keep important travel documents safe.
