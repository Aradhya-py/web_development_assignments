Understanding Bank Accounts

A bank account helps people manage income, savings and everyday transactions in an organized way.

Saving regularly and tracking expenses can help build responsible financial habits.

Savings Account

A savings account is designed for storing money while providing access to common banking services.

Current Account

A current account is generally used for frequent transactions and business-related banking activities.

“Good financial decisions begin with understanding how your money works.”

Loans & Credit Education

Personal Loans

Personal loans can provide funds for eligible expenses. Borrowers should understand interest, fees and repayment obligations.

Responsible Borrowing

Before borrowing, compare the total cost and ensure that the repayment amount fits within your planned budget.

Financial Notice

Interest rates, fees, eligibility and approval decisions depend on the product and the applicant's circumstances.

Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Track regular expenses.
  • Maintain an emergency fund.
  • Compare financial products before choosing.
  • Read terms and conditions carefully.
  • Avoid unnecessary borrowing.
  • Pay bills and repayments on time.

Finance Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage used to calculate interest on a financial product.
EMI
A fixed periodic payment used to repay certain loans.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or deposited.
Tenure
The period for which a loan or financial product remains active.
