Welcome to the National Tech & Career Expo

The National Tech & Career Expo brings students, technology companies, professionals and educators together to explore career opportunities and industry trends.

Discover opportunities, learn from professionals and connect with organizations offering internships and placements.

Organizer Message

Our goal is to help students understand the technology industry, develop career awareness and connect with organizations that offer meaningful opportunities.

“The right opportunity can turn knowledge into a successful career.”

Expo Information

Students should carry their registration details and arrive before their selected sessions begin.

Explore university opportunities and visit the Microsoft careers website for additional career information.

Expo Preparation Tips

  • Complete registration before attending.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Prepare questions for speakers.
  • Carry an updated resume.
  • Attend relevant workshops.
  • Network professionally with participants.
  • Review opportunities after the expo.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the expo.
  2. Check the event schedule.
  3. Explore participating organizations.
  4. Attend selected workshops.
  5. Listen to industry speakers.
  6. Connect with companies and professionals.
  7. Explore internship and placement opportunities.

Expo Terms

Career Expo
An event connecting students with organizations and career opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning and work experience with an organization.
Placement
A career opportunity offered to eligible candidates through recruitment.
Workshop
A practical session focused on learning a specific skill or topic.
Certification
A document recognizing successful completion of a learning program.
Networking
The process of building professional connections and relationships.
