Important Terminology

Eligibility
The mandatory academic benchmarks and aggregate percentages required for admission into a specific course.
Merit List
An official ranking of applicants based on entrance examination scores (like GLAET) or qualifying academic performance.
Scholarship
Tuition fee concessions extended to meritorious students based on board exam scores or entrance test performance.
Application Fee
A mandatory registration charge to process candidate credentials for entrance screening and counseling.
