Medical Terminology Guide

Consultation
An initial diagnostic meeting between a patient and a physician to discuss symptoms and treatment plans.
Follow-up
A scheduled subsequent visit to review recovery progress and evaluate medication efficacy.
OPD (Outpatient Department)
Hospital division designed for patients receiving medical care without formal overnight admission.
Appointment
A reserved time slot allocated for a specific patient to consult with a medical specialist.

Patient Advisory: Seasonal Health Precautions

With seasonal changes approaching, our clinical experts recommend maintaining adequate hydration, practicing routine hygiene, and keeping emergency contact numbers handy. Chemical formula note for hydration tracking: $H_2O$ intake is vital. Water purity level index $\text{ppm} \le 50$ is recommended for drinking fountains.
