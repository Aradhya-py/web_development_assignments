Event Overview & Objectives

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 brings together over 100 top-tier employers, global universities, and thousands of ambitious job seekers under one roof. Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic talent and industry demand.

"This event is the premier networking platform for matching exceptional graduate talent with leading global corporations."

— Event Organizing Committee

Attendee Preparation Guide

Make the most of your fair experience by following these practical preparation steps.

  • Print at least 20 copies of your updated, professional resume.
  • Dress in formal business attire and arrive early to beat morning rushes.
  • Research participating companies and prepare targeted questions.
  • Practice a concise 60-second self-introduction pitch.
  1. Register online and download your digital entry badge.
  2. Plan your schedule around key panel discussions and employer talks.
  3. Attend resume review and interview skill workshops early in the day.
  4. Follow up with recruiter contacts via email within 48 hours post-event.

Terminology & FAQs

Career Fair
An employment exposition where job seekers and employers meet face-to-face to discuss opportunities.
Internship
A professional learning experience offering practical work experience in a specific field.
Placement
The formal recruitment process of placing graduating students into permanent job roles.
Scholarship
Financial funding grants offered by universities and institutions for higher education.
Apprenticeship
A combination of on-the-job training and classroom instruction under skilled mentors.
