Admission Overview

GLA University welcomes students who are interested in building a strong academic and professional future. The admission process is designed to help eligible students select suitable programs and complete their applications successfully.

Eligibility

Applicants must satisfy the academic eligibility requirements of their chosen program. Students should check the official admission guidelines before submitting their application.

Application Process

  1. Visit the official university admission website.
  2. Select the desired program.
  3. Complete the online application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the application before the deadline.

Required Documents

  • Class 10 marksheet
  • Class 12 marksheet
  • Transfer certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Valid identity proof
  • Migration certificate if applicable

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum academic requirements required for admission.
Merit List
A list of applicants prepared according to their academic or entrance performance.
Counselling
A process where eligible students receive guidance and select available programs.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students according to specified criteria.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling as a student of the university.
