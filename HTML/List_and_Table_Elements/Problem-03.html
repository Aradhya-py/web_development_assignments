Faculty Information

Faculty members guide students through lectures, practical sessions, projects and academic activities.

Academic Rules

  • Attend classes regularly.
  • Maintain classroom discipline.
  • Submit assignments before the deadline.
  • Carry the required academic materials.
  • Follow laboratory safety instructions.

Practical Class Preparation

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Review the required theory.
  3. Prepare the necessary code or experiment.
  4. Attend the practical session on time.
  5. Submit the practical work.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session focused on theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students apply concepts using tools and equipment.
Tutorial
A smaller academic session focused on practice and problem solving.
Internal Assessment
Assessment conducted during the semester to evaluate student progress.
Practical
A session where students perform hands-on academic activities.

Mandatory: Students must maintain the required attendance and complete all academic activities.

Important Academic Notice: Timetables and examination schedules may change according to university requirements.

The department encourages students to combine classroom learning with practical projects and continuous self-learning.
