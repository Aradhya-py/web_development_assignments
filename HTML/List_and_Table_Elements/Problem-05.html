Annual College Fest 2026

Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day celebration featuring cultural activities, technical competitions, sports, music, dance and creative events for students.

Registration Process

  1. Visit the official fest registration page.
  2. Select the competition you want to join.
  3. Enter your participant details.
  4. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  5. Submit the registration form.
  6. Save your registration confirmation.

Registration Deadline: 5 October 2026

Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college identity card.
  • Participants must report to the venue before the scheduled event.
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice is prohibited.
  • Participants must follow coordinator instructions.
  • Mandatory: All participants must complete registration before the deadline.

Event Terms

Open Event
An event that is open to eligible students without a fixed team requirement.
Team Event
An event in which participants compete as a group.
Individual Event
An event where one participant competes independently.
Registration
The process of officially entering a competition or fest activity.
Coordinator
The person responsible for organizing and managing an event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where finalists compete for the winning position.
